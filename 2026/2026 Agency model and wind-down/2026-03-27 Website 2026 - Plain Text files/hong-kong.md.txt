## Company Focus Summary & Brief History

We have worked extensively in smart-city and industrial domains thanks to our unique engineering advantage. Our team’s background allows us to deeply understand operational processes across multiple industries and regions, based on many years of hands-on experience in North America and Southeast Asia.

Ambient Systems was incorporated in Singapore on July 5, 2021 (closed 2025) after receiving pre-seed investment from the National University of Singapore Graduate Research and Innovation Programme (NUS GRIP). The company has roots dating back to 2020 when founder Ivan Damnjanovic first proposed sensor- and AI-driven solutions for smart homes, healthcare facilities, and sustainable building management.

In 2023 we incorporated in Hong Kong as the Asia delivery node for high-ticket advisory services. In 2024 we incorporated in Quebec, Canada (Solutions Flux D’IA Inc.) as the headquarters for the live Ambient SaaS product. Both current entities are actively licensed the core IP and optimization models held personally by the founder (Serbia entity).
