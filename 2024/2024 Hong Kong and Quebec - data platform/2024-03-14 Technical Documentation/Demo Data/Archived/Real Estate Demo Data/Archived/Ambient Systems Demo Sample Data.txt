Financial Ratios:
	0.	Net Operating Income (NOI):
	•	NOI = Gross Rental Income - Operating Expenses
	•	Represents the property's income after deducting operating expenses but before debt service and taxes. It's a key indicator of the property's profitability.
	0.	Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate):
	•	Cap Rate = NOI / Property Value
	•	Measures the property's annual return on investment based on its current income. Cap rates are used to compare different properties and assess their investment potential.
	0.	Internal Rate of Return (IRR):
	•	IRR is the rate of return that makes the net present value (NPV) of all cash flows from the investment equal to zero. It represents the property's annualized rate of return over the investment period.
	0.	Cash-on-Cash Return (CoC):
	•	CoC = Annual Cash Flow / Equity Investment
	•	Measures the annual return on the equity investment in the property. It helps investors evaluate the cash flow generated relative to the amount of equity invested.
	0.	Debt Yield:
	•	Debt Yield = NOI / Loan Amount
	•	Indicates the property's ability to cover its debt obligations. Lenders use debt yield to assess the risk associated with providing a loan.
	0.	Loan Constant:
	•	Loan Constant = Annual Debt Service / Loan Amount
	•	Represents the annual debt service payment as a percentage of the loan amount. It helps assess the affordability of debt financing for the property.
	0.	Rentable Square Footage (RSF) Efficiency:
	•	RSF Efficiency = Rentable Square Feet / Gross Square Feet
	•	Measures the efficiency of space utilization within the property. Higher RSF efficiency ratios indicate more efficient use of space and potentially higher rental income per square foot.
	0.	Expense Recovery Ratio:
	•	Expense Recovery Ratio = Total Recoverable Expenses / Total Operating Expenses
	•	Indicates the percentage of operating expenses that can be recovered from tenants through expense reimbursements or common area maintenance (CAM) charges.

	•	Desirable Metrics: Energy efficiency, cost savings, sustainability.
	•	IoT Application: IoT sensors monitor energy consumption in real-time, helping to optimize building systems (HVAC, lighting, etc.) for energy efficiency. This leads to reduced operating expenses (OPEX), positively impacting metrics like NOI and cash flow margin.
	•	Financial Tie-in: Energy efficiency directly affects operating expenses (OPEX) by reducing utility costs. Lower energy expenses contribute to higher net operating income (NOI) and cash flow margin.
	2.	Predictive Maintenance:
	•	Desirable Metrics: Asset reliability, downtime reduction, maintenance costs.
	•	IoT Application: Predictive maintenance using IoT sensors helps prevent equipment failures and reduce unplanned downtime. This improves asset performance, reduces repair costs, and enhances tenant satisfaction, ultimately contributing to NOI growth and tenant retention.
	•	Financial Tie-in: Predictive maintenance reduces repair and replacement costs, minimizing capital expenditures (CAPEX) and preserving asset value. Lower maintenance expenses positively impact NOI and ROI
	3.	Occupant Comfort and Productivity:
	•	Desirable Metrics: Tenant satisfaction, productivity, lease renewal rates.
	•	IoT Application: IoT devices adjust environmental conditions based on occupancy and preferences, enhancing tenant comfort and productivity. Satisfied tenants are more likely to renew leases, reducing vacancy rates and improving overall property performance.

	•	IoT Application: IoT sensors track space utilization patterns, allowing property managers to optimize layouts and allocate resources efficiently. Maximizing space usage increases rental income potential and improves key metrics like occupancy rate and ROI.
	•	Financial Tie-in: Maximizing space usage increases rental income potential by maximizing rentable area utilization. Higher rental income improves revenue-related metrics like NOI and ROI.
	5.	Security and Access Control:
	•	Desirable Metrics: Property security, tenant safety, risk management.
	•	IoT Application: IoT-based security systems enhance property safety by monitoring premises, controlling access, and providing real-time alerts. Ensuring a secure environment reduces potential risks and liabilities, safeguarding property value and tenant satisfaction.
	•	Financial Tie-in: Ensuring property security reduces potential losses from theft, vandalism, or liability claims. Lower security-related expenses contribute to higher NOI and profitability.
